# perception_cam: planted faults

Not copied into workspaces (it sits next to the fixture dir, not inside it). Generated by
`tools/make_fixtures.py perception_cam`; every number below is reproducible (byte-identical regeneration).

Setup: 5 scenarios x 80 frames (10 Hz, 8 s). Each `outputs/<name>/replay.mcap` has 80 messages on each of
`/camera/front/image`, `/perception/detections`, `/perception/tracks`, `/perception/latency`, `/diagnostics`.
Numbers are from `perception/eval_utils.match` (greedy, class-aware, IoU >= 0.5), with detections and labels joined on
`header.stamp` == label `stamp`. Frame k has stamp = recording start + k * 100 ms.

| scenario | recording start (UTC) | precision | recall | ID switches | e2e p50 / p95 / max (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| day_parking_lot | 2026-09-14 15:02:10 | 1.000 | 0.959 | 0 | 54.8 / 59.2 / 62.4 |
| night_parking_lot | 2026-09-14 21:40:00 | 1.000 | 0.672 | 1 | 48.9 / 54.7 / 59.5 |
| rain_crosswalk | 2026-09-17 08:15:30 | 0.988 | 0.946 | 0 | 50.4 / **125.6** / 140.3 |
| crowded_loading_dock | 2026-09-18 11:05:00 | 1.000 | 0.906 | **16** | 54.1 / 59.1 / 61.5 |
| highway_merge_far | 2026-09-21 13:30:45 | 1.000 | **0.414** | 0 | 45.6 / 50.6 / 57.0 |

## 1. Range: far objects missed (highway_merge_far)

The detector's recall decays exponentially past 38 m (falloff 7 m). Only highway_merge_far has objects beyond 40 m.
Recall by GT range, highway_merge_far:

| range | recall |
|---|---|
| 10-20 m | 40/43 = 0.93 |
| 20-30 m | 115/117 = 0.98 |
| 30-40 m | 77/80 = 0.96 |
| 40-50 m | 18/80 = 0.23 |
| 50-60 m | 12/138 = 0.09 |
| 60-70 m | 2/102 = 0.02 |
| 70-80 m | 1/80 = 0.01 |

Coarse bins: <20 m 0.930, 20-40 m 0.975, 40-60 m 0.138, >=60 m 0.016. By class: car 0.510, truck 0.125
(the trucks are at 46 m and 62-66 m). Every other scenario has all objects within 40 m.

## 2. Night: pedestrian recall collapses (night_parking_lot vs day_parking_lot)

When mean frame luminance is below 60 (night frames), pedestrian recall drops to 0.22 per frame and cyclist recall to
0.5. Car and truck recall stay at 0.97.

| class | day_parking_lot | night_parking_lot |
|---|---|---|
| pedestrian | 157/160 = 0.981 | 58/240 = 0.242 |
| car | 351/371 = 0.946 | 339/351 = 0.966 |

Side effect: tentative tracks die on their first miss, so night pedestrians rarely get a confirmed track
(2 short pedestrian tracks in the whole drive). Most of night's 5 track gaps come from those 2 tracks.

## 3. Tracking: ID switches and flicker (crowded_loading_dock)

Six workers cross in front of the vehicle in opposite directions at 8.5-12.6 m (crossings around frames 25, 47 and 65).
The boxes overlap heavily. Detector NMS (IoU 0.3, class-wise) and occlusion (>60% covered: recall x0.2) drop one of
each overlapping pair for a few frames. The greedy constant-position IoU tracker then swaps IDs or restarts tracks.

- ID switches (a GT object's matched track_id changes): **16**. The other scenarios have 0-1 (night 1, others 0).
- Pedestrian track IDs: 11 published IDs for 6 pedestrians. Day: 1 track per object.
- Flicker: 7 gaps in published tracks (a track_id disappears and comes back), and 14 tracked-to-untracked transitions
  for GT objects. Day has 2 and 2, highway 0 and 1, rain 3 and 3, night 5 and 7 (night: see fault 2).
- Pedestrian detection recall is 0.887 here, against 0.981 in day_parking_lot (occlusion/NMS). Trucks are at 0.963.

## 4. Latency: burst over the 100 ms budget (rain_crosswalk)

Heavy rain in frames 35-52 (08:15:33.5 to 08:15:35.2 UTC, 1.8 s) turns on the derain pre-filter (+70 ms). All 18 of
those frames, and no others, exceed the 100 ms end-to-end budget (107.7-140.3 ms; the rest of the drive peaks at 55.7 ms). On the same 18 frames
`/diagnostics` goes WARN: `status[0].level` 1, message `e2e latency <x> ms over 100 ms budget`, value `derain=true`.
Every other message in every scenario is level 0 / `OK`.

rain_crosswalk e2e: mean 65.8, p50 50.4, **p95 125.6**, max 140.3 ms. Other scenarios' p95: day 59.2, night 54.7,
crowded 59.1, highway 50.6 (max anywhere else: 62.4).

## 5. False-positive burst: phantom pedestrian (rain_crosswalk)

Glare off a puddle (a saturated blob in the image) shows up in frames 61-65 (08:15:36.1 to 08:15:36.5 UTC, 0.5 s).
It fires one pedestrian detection per frame with no matching label:
bbox `{x_min 1412, y_min 740, x_max 1448, y_max 840}`, range 9.14 m, score 0.556-0.626.
These are the only 5 unmatched detections in the fixture, so rain_crosswalk precision is 0.988 and every other
scenario is 1.000. The tracker confirms it as a phantom pedestrian track (published for frames 63-66, including one
coasting frame).

## 6. Nominal elsewhere

day_parking_lot is the baseline: recall 0.959, precision 1.000, 0 ID switches, p95 latency 59.2 ms, no WARN.
Minor, expected noise: about 3% random misses per object-frame. In rain_crosswalk the crossing pedestrians briefly
occlude the two parked cars (12 car-frames >60% covered, car recall 0.919).
